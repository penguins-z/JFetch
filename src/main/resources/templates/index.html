New search

Jobs already in the database (any status) are ignored. The search requests one page (about 10 jobs, one billed request) at a time until it has that many × 10 new jobs, JSearch runs out of results, or it reaches pages (the per-search cost cap). New jobs that match the keyword and experience range are stored as Pending; the rest as Criteria failed. Repeating the exact same search is served from the cache until you use "Skip all pending", which expires it.
